Part 1: Advanced Environmental Collocations

Dire environmental consequences

Band 8.0+

Định nghĩa: Hậu quả môi trường thảm khốc/nghiêm trọng.

"The unchecked deforestation of the rainforest could lead to dire environmental consequences."

Sử dụng thay cho: "Bad environmental problems".

Bolster punitive measures

Action Phrase

Định nghĩa: Tăng cường/Củng cố các biện pháp trừng phạt.

"The government decided to bolster punitive measures by increasing fines for illegal logging."
Pro Tip: Dùng cực kỳ hiệu quả trong Body 2 khi đề xuất giải pháp của Chính phủ.

Lenient environmental regulations

Criticism

Định nghĩa: Các quy định môi trường lỏng lẻo/nhân nhượng.

"Lenient environmental regulations have contributed to increased pollution levels."

Trái nghĩa: Stringent environmental regulations.

Sustainable manufacturing processes

Solution

Định nghĩa: Các quy trình sản xuất bền vững.

"Businesses need to adopt more sustainable manufacturing processes to minimize waste."

Keyword cho chủ đề Production/Manufacturing.

Part 2: Model Essay & Structural Analysis

The Essay Topic:

"The surge in manufacturing consumer goods has led to environmental damage. Discuss the reasons and propose solutions."

Sơ đồ Tư duy (Problem - Solution)

graph TD A[Surge in Production] --> B(Reason 1: Chemical By-products) A --> C(Reason 2: Disposable Products) B --> D[Pollution of Air/Water] C --> E[Landfills & Habitats Loss] D --> F{SOLUTIONS} E --> F F --> G[Companies: Eco-friendly materials] F --> H[Government: Tax incentives/Sustainable laws]

Teacher's Note by Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư: Lưu ý cấu trúc bài viết Cause-Solution. Bạn cần đảm bảo sự tương quan (logic mapping) giữa nguyên nhân ở Body 1 và giải pháp ở Body 2. Nếu bạn nói về "disposable products", hãy đề xuất "reusable alternatives".

Word count: 305 | Band: 7.0+ SAMPLE ESSAY

In the modern era, the surge in manufacturing consumer goods has led to heightened environmental damage. I will outline various factors contributing to this problem and propose potential measures to address it.

To begin, the escalation in consumer goods production exerts a dual negative impact on the environment. Firstly, there is the emission of chemical by-products during the manufacturing process, and secondly, the proliferation of disposable products. As the volume of goods manufactured increases, so does the release of toxic waste and emissions from factories into the environment. This results in the pollution of water sources and the air, leading to the deaths of numerous aquatic and terrestrial animals. Moreover, in response to growing consumer demands, an influx of single-use products is introduced, many of which are non-biodegradable. Despite their short lifespans, these items persist as waste for thousands of years, transforming our planet into an extensive landfill and endangering the habitats of various species.

Swift actions are imperative to mitigate the adverse environmental impacts stemming from the escalating production of consumer goods. First and foremost, companies should champion the use of eco-friendlier materials. A notable example is the prominent coffee chain Starbucks, which recently replaced plastic straws with reusable alternatives crafted from materials such as paper or bamboo. Moreover, governments need to actively encourage the adoption of more sustainable manufacturing processes. For instance, many U.S. states provide tax incentives and benefits to businesses that employ renewable energy sources, with some companies even being offered the opportunity to procure green energy at lower costs than conventional fossil fuels.

In conclusion, the rise in consumer goods production has led to severe environmental repercussions. To address this issue, collaborative efforts between governments and corporations are necessary to transition production methods towards more environmentally sustainable practices, including the adoption of greener materials.

2. Conventional fossil fuels --- Năng lượng truyền thống (Than đá, dầu mỏ)
Kiểm tra Đáp án
  • Heightened environmental damage: Tình trạng môi trường bị tàn phá nặng nề hơn.
  • Conventional fossil fuels: Các loại nhiên liệu như than đá, khí đốt gây ô nhiễm cao.
  • Renewable energy: Năng lượng từ gió, mặt trời, thủy điện.
