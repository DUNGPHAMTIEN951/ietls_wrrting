Topic 1: Professional & Competitive Sport

Professional Sport Arguments

  • ✔ It's a multi-million-pound industry providing jobs.
  • ✔ Top stars entertain millions and deserve to monetize their talent.
  • ✔ High standards push athletes beyond amateur levels.

Competitive Nature

  • ● Natural Instinct: Competition is inherent in human nature.
  • ● Safe Outlet: A healthy way to release energy and aggression.
  • ● Community: Creates a sense of belonging for fans.
Debate: Are Sports Salaries Too High?
VIEWPOINT A: SALARIES ARE TOO HIGH VIEWPOINT B: SALARIES ARE FAIR
Vital professionals (doctors, engineers) are undervalued compared to athletes. Athletes have dedicated hours of rigorous practice to reach the top.
Athletes do not provide a "vital service" to society. Careers are short (often only lasting 10 years).
Salaries should be compatible with average wages. Television rights and sponsorships attract huge viewers and revenue.

Teacher's Note: Vocabulary focus

Khi viết về chủ đề lương bổng, thay vì chỉ dùng "money", hãy sử dụng các cụm từ academic như: Enormous salaries, lucrative contracts, multi-million-pound industry, hoặc monetary rewards. Để so sánh, hãy dùng cấu trúc "Compatible with..." (Tương xứng với...).

Television: Pros & Cons

Pros:

A way to wind down after work, educational documentaries, news coverage.

Cons:

Sedentary lifestyle (less time playing), exposure to violence, addictive nature.

Mobile Phones

Key Benefits:

Stay in touch anywhere, fashion accessories, multifunctional (GPS, Camera, Internet).

Key Drawbacks:

Dangerous distraction while driving, disturbance in public places, potential brain damage from waves.

Pro Tip by Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư

Trong dạng bài "Discuss both views" về Internet và báo chí, hãy nhấn mạnh sự khác biệt về chất lượng: "Newspaper articles are written by professionals, producing better quality writing than amateurs on the Internet."

Topic 3: International Tourism

Economic & Social Impacts

Positive Effects (Lợi ích)

  • Economic Vitality: Creates employment in accommodation, transport, and entertainment.
  • Cultural Exchange: Opens our minds and allows us to experience different cultures.
  • Infrastructure: Attracts investment from multi-national companies, improving local standards of living.

Negative Effects (Tác động xấu)

  • Environmental Damage: Building facilities destroys habitats; beaches are spoilt by hotels.
  • Resource Pressure: Puts excessive pressure on local resources (water, food).
  • Cultural Erosion: Local traditions and cultures may be endangered or commercialized.

Luyện tập Vocabulary (Gap Fill)

Điền từ thích hợp vào chỗ trống (Dựa trên kiến thức bài học):

1. Many people believe that the benefits of mobile phones ________ the drawbacks.

2. Watching TV is a common way to ________ after a hard day at work.

3. High salaries for athletes are justified because they have ________ hours of practice to reach the top.

1. Outweigh (Lớn hơn/vượt qua)

2. Wind down / Relax (Thư giãn)

3. Dedicated (Cống hiến/Dành ra)
