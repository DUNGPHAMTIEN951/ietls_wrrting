International Trade Vocabulary

Business & Commerce

  • Domestic business Doanh nghiệp trong nước
  • International commerce Thương mại quốc tế
  • Foreign partnership Đối tác nước ngoài
  • Tariffs Thuế quan

Technical Phrases

  • Technical proficiency Độ thành thạo kỹ thuật
  • On a local level Ở mức độ địa phương
  • Unrestricted trade TM tự do không giới hạn
  • Economic integration Hội nhập kinh tế
🎓 Teacher's Note:

Khi bàn về International Trade, hãy chú ý phân biệt giữa Tariffs (thuế đánh vào hàng nhập khẩu) và Taxes (thuế nói chung). Việc sử dụng chính xác thuật ngữ kinh tế sẽ nâng điểm Lexical Resource lên Band 8.0+.

Task 1: Chart Analysis (Age & Internet Use)

graph TD A[Internet Use Australia 2010-2011] --> B[Older Groups] A --> C[Younger Groups] B --> B1(Emailing & Paying Bills: 70-80%) C --> C1(Social Media: ~100%) C1 --> D{Trend} D -->|Decrease with Age| E[Over 65s: ~25%]

Dàn bài (Structure)

  • Intro: Paraphrase đề bài.
  • Overview: Older groups vs Younger groups trend.
  • Body 1: Emailing & Bills.
  • Body 2: Social Media.

Estimated Score

CC:9.0
Overall:8.0
Sample Band 8.0

Biểu đồ người dùng Australia (2010 - 2011)

The bar charts illustrate the percentage of users in Australia from different age groups who used the internet for emailing, paying bills, and social media in 2010 and 2011.

Overall, it can be seen that while paying bills and using email was more common among the older age groups, the figures for social media showed the opposite trend.

For both emailing and paying bills, the highest use rate was found among the ‘older than 65’ age group, at approximately 80%...

The use of social media appears to have declined with age in the measured years. While almost 100% of ‘18-25’ year olds used social media, only around a quarter of those aged over 65 used the internet for the same purpose.

Contrast: "While [X] was more common, [Y] showed the opposite trend."
Range: "The figures for the older groups ranged between 70-80%."
Highest: "The highest use rate was found among the [Group]."
Follow-up: "Email was overall the most common activity, followed by paying bills."

Task 2: Urbanization Problems & Solutions

● Topic Analysis

Vấn đề của giới trẻ tại các siêu đô thị lớn.

Q1.

What are the problems that young people living in those cities are facing?

Q2.

Suggest some solutions.

"Giới trẻ thường đối mặt với: Chi phí sống (Cost of living) và Sức khỏe tinh thần (Mental Health). Giải pháp cần hướng đến Chính phủ (Government-led)."

Problem 1

Rising Cost of Living

Meagre budgets, low-paying jobs.

Solution

Relocate universities to satellite cities to reduce housing demand.

Problem 2

Poor Mental Health

Overwhelming schedules, career stress.

Solution

Governments should promote a culture of awareness and stress-relief activities.

✍️ Thử Thách Thực Hành Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư

Bài 1: Viết đoạn văn triển khai câu chủ đề sau:

“Work-life imbalance is a major problem that many young people are facing today.”

Gợi ý từ vựng 8.0 cho bài thực hành:

Career ladder Compete with one another Financially stable Stress-relieving activities Overwhelming

Bài 2: Viết Essay hoàn chỉnh:

"People living in large cities today face many problems in their everyday life. What are these problems? Should governments encourage people to move to smaller regional towns?"
