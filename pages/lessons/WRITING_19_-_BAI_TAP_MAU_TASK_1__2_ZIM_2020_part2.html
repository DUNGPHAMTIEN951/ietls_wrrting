Teacher's Note: Đối với bảng số liệu có thời gian, cần tập trung vào: (1) Xu hướng chung (tăng/giảm), (2) Điểm nổi bật nhất (Japan là "market leader"), (3) Sự nghịch thường (Saudi Arabia giảm mạnh).

Kiến thức trọng tâm (Key Concepts)

The reverse ~ The opposite

Sử dụng để chỉ sự tương phản hoàn toàn. Ví dụ: A tăng, trong khi "the reverse" (điều ngược lại) xảy ra với B.

Revenue & Earnings

Dùng thay thế cho "export values" để tránh lặp từ (lexical resource).

Consecutive years

Số năm liên tiếp. "Japan topped the list in three consecutive years."

Bài mẫu Band 8.0+ | Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư

The table shows changes in the export values of kiwi fruit from New Zealand to five nations from 2010 to 2012.

Overall, the value of exports of kiwi to Saudi Arabia dropped significantly over the course of three years, while the reverse was seen in the remaining countries. Additionally, the exports of kiwi to Japan generated the biggest amount of money in three consecutive years.

The value of exports to Japan topped the list in 2010, at more than 270 million dollars, nearly 3 times higher than the figure for China and 10 times higher than that of Saudi Arabia. Meanwhile, exports to Mexico and Russia brought much smaller revenues, with NZ$1.2 million and NZ$968,000 respectively.

By 2012, the value of exports to Japan had risen considerably, and reached over NZ$325 million in value. The figures for China, Mexico and Russia also saw increases to NZ$94 million, NZ$3.3 million and NZ$2.4 million respectively. On the other hand, the value of exports to Saudi Arabia dropped to only NZ$106,000 by 2011, before ending up at NZ$82,000 in 2012.

Chủ đề 2: Social Media vs Face-to-face Interaction

Dạng bài: Advantages vs Disadvantages (Outweigh?)

Sơ đồ Tư duy (Ideation Map)

graph TD A[Social Media Trends] --> B(Advantages) A --> C(Disadvantages) B --> B1[Global Connection] B --> B2[Free Educational Access] C --> C1[Online Bullying/Abuse] C --> C2[Mental Health Issues] C --> C3[Relationship Erosion] style B fill:#dcfce7,stroke:#16a34a style C fill:#fee2e2,stroke:#dc2626

Vocabulary Highlights

  • Infiltrate (v): Thâm nhập sâu vào cuộc sống.
  • Facilitate communication: Thúc đẩy/tạo điều kiện cho giao tiếp.
  • Fall prey to (idiom): Trở thành nạn nhân của...
  • Toxic environment: Môi trường độc hại.
  • Reclusive users: Người sống khép kín/ngại giao tiếp thực.
Pro Tip by Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư: Trong dạng "Outweigh", bạn phải làm rõ bên nào mạnh hơn. Ở bài mẫu này, tác giả chọn Disadvantages là yếu tố quan trọng hơn (concerning) trong dài hạn.

High-Scoring Essay Sample Word count: ~295 words

Social media has deeply infiltrated everyone’s life and is believed to be replacing our face-to-face interaction. This situation, although advantageous in certain aspects, is generally a detriment to true human communication in the long run.

On the one hand, there are a number of benefits from using social networks to communicate. Firstly, they facilitate communication in modern times as people can now globally connect with old friends and relatives. For example, Facebook currently provides service for 2.4 billion users. Secondly, study sessions frequently occur on social networking websites through live streaming services. Therefore, learners have free access to online classes.

On the other hand, the disadvantages are much more concerning. People can fall prey to online communication abuse, such as bullying and harassment. Social networking websites can become a toxic environment where users are verbally abused because rules are often loose. Furthermore, overuse can lead to people downplaying real-life relationships. Nowadays, many reclusive users prefer living in a virtual world, which may cause serious mental effects like anxiety and loneliness.

In conclusion, the downsides of social media replacing face-to-face interaction are more significant than the benefits users could reap from those sites.
