Pro Tip: Collocation Usage

Đừng dùng "men and women work different jobs". Hãy nâng cấp lên:

"The distribution of job types between genders often stems from distinct physical and mental strengths rather than mere discrimination."

Exercise: Fill in the blanks with the correct collocation

"The rapid (1) ________ of information today has led to (2) ________, causing many people to feel overwhelmed. To counter this, governments should (3) ________ public awareness campaigns."

Check Answers
1. Explosion / Proliferation
2. Information overload
3. Implement / Carry out
