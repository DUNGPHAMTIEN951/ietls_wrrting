Chủ đề 1: Career Path & Job Satisfaction

TOPIC: CAREER

The Writing Prompt

"Some people know from an early age what career they want to pursue... To what extent do you agree? What other things can people do to have a satisfying working life?"

graph TD A[Career Satisfaction] --> B[Option A: Single Path] A --> C[Option B: Diversification] B --> B1[Long-term goals] B --> B2[Expertise/Rewarding] C --> C1[Reinvigorating experience] C --> C2[Salary/Life balance] C --> C3[Working Environment]

Introduction: It is true that some people know from an early age what career they want to pursue... While I accept that this may suit many people, I believe that others enjoy changing careers or seeking job satisfaction in different ways.

📌 Teacher's Note: Đây là dạng bài Two-part Question. Bạn cần trả lời rõ cả 2 câu hỏi ở phần Mở bài để đảm bảo điểm Task Response cao.

Body 1 (Agreement): On the one hand, having a defined career path can certainly lead to a satisfying working life. Many people decide as young children what they want to do as adults... For example, many children dream of becoming doctors... very few people who have qualified as doctors choose to change career because they find their work so rewarding.

Body 2 (Other Factors): On the other hand, people find happiness in their working lives in different ways. Firstly... starting out on a completely new career path can be a reinvigorating experience. Secondly, some see their jobs as simply a means of earning money... Finally, job satisfaction is often the result of working conditions, such as an inspirational boss or positive atmosphere.

Từ vựng/Cấu trúc Giải thích & Ngữ cảnh
Defined career path Lộ trình sự nghiệp xác định. Dùng thay cho "job history".
Realise this ambition Thực hiện hóa tham vọng. Kết hợp (Collocation) cực tốt thay cho "do dream".
A means of earning money Một phương tiện kiếm tiền (Cách nói nhấn mạnh công việc không phải đam mê).
Regardless of the profession Bất kể nghề nghiệp nào. Cấu trúc nhượng bộ (Concession).

Chủ đề 2: Unpaid Work (Community Service)

Phạm Tiến Dũng Pro Tip

Khi thảo luận về trẻ em/thanh thiếu niên, luôn cân nhắc khía cạnh "Academic Pressure". Đây là "vũ khí" lập luận cực mạnh trong các bài Agree/Disagree về giáo dục.

Structure Idea (Against Compulsory Work)

  • School is already demanding (homework, revision).
  • Need time for sports/leisure to balance mental health.
  • Forced volunteerism leads to resentment.
  • Infringes on values of a free and fair society.

Expert Sample Extract (Band 8.0+)

"...In fact, I would argue that it goes against the values of a free and fair society to force a group of people to do something against their will. Doing this can only lead to resentment amongst young people..."

Luyện tập Vocabulary:

Điền từ thích hợp vào chỗ trống: Compulsory, Resentment, Volunteer, Beneficial

1. Mandatory service can lead to ________.
2. Work should be on a ________ basis.

1. Resentment | 2. Volunteer/Non-compulsory

Chủ đề 3: Animal Testing (The "Necessary Evil")

Balanced Viewpoint Strategy

Arguments Against

  • ✘ Morally wrong to cause suffering.
  • ✘ Lives of all creatures should be respected.
  • ✘ Benefits do not justify the trauma.

Arguments For (Medical)

  • ✔ No reliable alternatives currently.
  • ✔ Human lives are more vital.
  • ✔ Crucial for vital medical research.

Key Philosophical Term: "A Necessary Evil"

Cụm từ này cực kỳ đắt giá khi nói về các chủ đề gây tranh cãi mà chúng ta buộc phải chấp nhận vì lợi ích lớn hơn. (Ví dụ: Animal testing, Nuclear power).
