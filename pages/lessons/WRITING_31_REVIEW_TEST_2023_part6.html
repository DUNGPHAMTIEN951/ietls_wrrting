Team Sports & Cooperation in Education

Prompt:

"Some people believe that the most effective way for children to learn how to cooperate with one another is to engage them in team sports at school. Discuss."

Perspective A: Agree

  • ✅ Communication: Verbal & non-verbal (body language, hand signals).
  • ✅ Shared Goals: Understanding common objectives vs. individual effort.
  • ✅ Execution: Strategic drills require immediate synchronization.

Perspective B: Disagree

  • ❌ Negative Impact: Pressure on less athletic children (low self-esteem).
  • ❌ Blame Culture: Immature students may blame peers for losses.
  • ❌ Alternatives: Video games or art-based collaborative projects.

Teacher's Note by Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư

Trong dạng bài Opinion (Agree or Disagree), các em có thể chọn hướng "Balanced" (Cân bằng) hoặc "Strongly Agree/Disagree". Tuy nhiên, việc phát triển từ vựng theo chủ đề (Topic-specific vocabulary) như athleticism, self-esteem, hay execute strategies là chìa khóa để chạm mốc Band 7.0+.

Advanced Vocabulary Focus

Task 1: Pie Chart - Time Allocation (1958 vs 2008)

pie title Time Allocation Comparison "Work (1958)" : 33 "Sleep (1958)" : 31 "Socializing (1958)" : 19 "Other (1958)" : 17

Pro Tip from Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư:

Khi mô tả Pie Chart có sự thay đổi theo thời gian, hãy sử dụng các động từ chỉ sự thay đổi mạnh mẽ như quadrupled (tăng gấp 4) hoặc cụm từ chỉ mối quan hệ inverse relationship (quan hệ nghịch đảo) để ghi điểm Task Response.

Key Trends (Xu hướng chính)

  • 📈 Working: Rose from 33% (one-third) to 42%.
  • 📉 Sleeping: Dropped from just under one-third to 25%.
  • 🚀 Commuting: Quadrupled from 2% to 8%.

Essential Structures

  • • there was an inverse relationship between A and B
  • • more time was allocated to [Activity]
  • • the figure for [X] quadrupled to [Y%]
  • • [X] took up the least percentage of time

"Playing sports can become highly competitive, and this may ________ children's willingness to cooperate."
