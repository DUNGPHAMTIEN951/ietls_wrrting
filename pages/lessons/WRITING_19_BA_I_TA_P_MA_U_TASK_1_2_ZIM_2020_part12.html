01 Writing Task 2: Travel vs. Media in Cultural Learning

1. Phân tích chiến thuật

graph TD A[Đề bài: Học văn hóa qua Media vs. Du lịch] --> B[Body 1: Lợi ích của Books/Internet] A --> C[Body 2: Tại sao Du lịch vẫn quan trọng hơn] B --> B1[Sách: Hình ảnh mô tả] B --> B2[Internet: Đa dạng góc nhìn] C --> C1[Trải nghiệm thực tế - First-hand] C --> C2[Thông tin cập nhật - Contemporary]

💡 Teacher's Note

Đây là dạng bài Opinion (Agree/Disagree) nhưng đề bài đưa ra hai vế so sánh. Hãy thừa nhận lợi ích của phương tiện truyền thông trước khi khẳng định giá trị cốt lõi của việc trải nghiệm trực tiếp.

🚀 Pro Tip

Sử dụng cụm "First-hand experience" và "Subjective viewpoints" để nâng điểm tiêu chí Lexical Resource lên Band 8.0+.

Model Essay (Band 8.0+)

Many people believe that books, movies, and the internet allow them to learn as much about culture as travelling to those countries would... However, while I admit that such sources of information can be effective, they fail to provide people with the latest information or practical experiences; therefore, people still need to travel to learn about culture.

On the one hand... people will have a better understanding of cultural traits(1)... giving more diverse information from different perspectives(2).

On the other hand... information might soon be out-of-date... people need to travel to learn what local people actually wear and speak in contemporary society(4). Additionally, people cannot get first-hand experience(5) when at home... they should get direct exposure to(6) the culture... without relying on others’ subjective viewpoints(7).

03 Writing Task 2: Cyclists & Drivers (Problems & Solutions)

The Problems

  • High mortality rate in collisions.
  • Inconvenience and traffic congestion for motor vehicles.

The Solutions

  • Implementation of strict speed limits (e.g., 50 km/h).
  • Construction of designated/separate lanes.

Key Vocabulary Collocations

Undesirable consequences
Mortality rate
Collide with
Take into consideration
Designated for
Simultaneously
