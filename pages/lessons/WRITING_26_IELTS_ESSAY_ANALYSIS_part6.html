Topic 11: Community Service for Teenagers

Question: Should high school students be required to do unpaid community service?

"...I do not believe that society has anything to gain from obliging young people to do unpaid work. In fact, I would argue that it goes against the values of a free and fair society to force a group of people to do something against their will. Doing this can only lead to resentment amongst young people, who would feel that they were being used... In conclusion, teenagers may choose to work for free and help others, but in my opinion, we should not make this compulsory."

Hobbies: Difficulty vs Enjoyment

Một số người cho rằng sở thích phải khó mới đem lại niềm vui. Bài mẫu Band 9 dưới đây bác bỏ quan điểm này bằng cách phân tích sự cân bằng giữa sở thích dễ và khó.

Type of Hobby Examples Benefit/Value
Easy Hobbies Swimming, Photography Relaxing, straightforward, satisfying results
Difficult Hobbies Film Editing Sense of achievement, high level of performance

Band 9 Sample Extract:

"If an activity is more challenging, we might feel a greater sense of satisfaction when we manage to do it successfully... many hobbies give us more pleasure when we reach a higher level of performance because the results are better and the feeling of achievement is greater."

Topic 15: Gender Equality

"Selection of university students should be based on merit, and it would be both impractical and unfair to change to a selection procedure based on gender."

Note: Use "Merit-based" instead of just "Ability" to sound more academic.

Kiểm tra kiến thức

Nối từ vựng với nghĩa tương ứng

1. "A reinvigorating experience" nghĩa là gì?
Đáp án: Một trải nghiệm mang lại năng lượng mới, sự tươi mới. Thường dùng khi nói về việc đổi ngành nghề hoặc đi du lịch.
2. Phân biệt "National borders" và "National boundaries"?
Đáp án: Hai từ này đồng nghĩa (Synonyms). Việc thay đổi giữa chúng giúp bài viết tránh lặp từ (repetition), một tiêu chí quan trọng trong Lexical Resource Band 8+.
