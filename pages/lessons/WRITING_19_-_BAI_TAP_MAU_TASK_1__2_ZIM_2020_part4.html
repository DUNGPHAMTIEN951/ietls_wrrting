Task 1: Process - Rainwater Harvesting

Bài Mẫu Band 8.0+

The diagram illustrates the process of harvesting rainwater in order to provide drinking water for residents in an Australian town.

Overall, there are approximately six stages in the process of rainwater harvesting(1), beginning with the collection of rain to the delivery of(2) treated potable(3) drinking water to each of the houses.

Firstly, rain is caught on the rooftops of houses and runs down through a system of connected drain pipes which lead to a water filter. Once the water has been filtered, it then moves through the pipes to a large storage tank.(4)

When the water is needed it is transferred through another pipe to a water treatment tank where it is treated with chemicals to make it safe to drink. Finally, once the water has been treated, it is then pumped(5) through another set of pipes that connects to each of the individuals houses where residents can access it through their water faucets(6).

Từ Vựng Chuyên Sâu

  • Potable: Có thể uống được (Trang trọng hơn 'drinkable').
  • Harvesting: Thu hoạch/Thu gom (Thường dùng cho tài nguyên).
  • Water Faucet: Vòi nước.

Pro Tip by Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư

Trong bài Process, hãy sử dụng linh hoạt các từ nối chỉ trình tự (Firstly, Once, Finally) kết hợp với cấu trúc bị động (Passive Voice) để tăng tính khách quan và học thuật.

Task 2: Essay - Art & Special Abilities

Sơ đồ Tư duy (Essay Structure)

graph TD A[Intro: Art for Everyone vs Talent Only] --> B[Body 1: Everyone can create Art] A --> C[Body 2: Talent makes it easier/better] B --> B1[Personal Expression] B --> B2[Subjectivity of Quality] C --> C1[Technical Mastery: Medium] C --> C2[Avoidance of Frustration] B1 & B2 & C1 & C2 --> D[Conclusion: Opinion]

Academic Vocabulary

Naturally gifted Năng khiếu bẩm sinh
Work of art Tác phẩm nghệ thuật
Unique expression Sự thể hiện độc đáo
Medium Hình thức nghệ thuật
Subjective Tính chủ quan

Sample Answer by Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư:

While some people believe that everyone has the ability to create art(1), others believe that only those with special talents(2) are able. Personally, I believe that everyone has the ability to create art, however some people are more naturally gifted(3) than others.

On one hand, creating art is something that everyone can do. However, not every painting will be a masterpiece, nor every song a hit. Whether it is a painting, a song, or a poem, art is the personal expression(4) of the artist, and its quality is subjective(5) to the viewer or listener. For example, while there are many people who believe that the Mona Lisa is a brilliant work of art(6), there are also many people who do not appreciate it...

However, creating art may be far easier or enjoyable for those who are naturally gifted or talented. Art is about expressing an idea, emotion, or feeling(8) via a specific medium(9). Many people simply become too frustrated and quickly give up(10) because they do not have the skill, while those blessed with certain skills are able to express their ideas with ease and grace.

In conclusion, although I believe that everyone has the ability to create art, many people who do not have the natural talent(11) are easily frustrated and give up. However, this does not mean they cannot create art; they simply lack patience and humility.
