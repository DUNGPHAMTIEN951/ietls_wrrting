UNIT 1

MAPS (Bản đồ)

Sample Question: Small Theatre Changes (2010 - 2012) Actual Test

(1) The plans illustrate how much a theater has changed between 2010 and 2012. It seems that the theater was expanded sideways to build more facilities.

Overall, a number of changes took place all over the layout of the building, (2) some of which involved the conversion and relocation of some features. The only exception was the center as it remained relatively unchanged.

In 2010, the theater could be entered from the main entrance to the South, through which (3) a cafe could be easily spotted in the right corner. A ticket office and administration office were situated opposite the cafe. After 2 years, the ticket office, however, was relocated to (4) the bottom right corner where the cafe had been demolished, while the administration office was moved to the back. This was to make way for a new restaurant area in the left rear corner.

The old media room and storage room had been moved to the top left and right side respectively, both of which could now be accessed from the new hallway. (5) The space which used to be occupied by the media room (6) was reduced by two times so that the stage in front could be expanded. Finally, a new showers area had been built next to the media room. Lastly, no changes were made to the central auditorium.

Expert Linguistic Analysis

1. Nominal Clauses with "How much"

Mẫu câu mở bài cực kỳ hiệu quả để paraphrase đề bài thay vì chỉ dùng "The changes in...".

Structure: The plans + illustrate + how much + Noun + has changed
Lưu ý: Đây là mệnh đề danh từ, không được đảo trợ động từ lên trước chủ ngữ.
❌ Sai: ...how much has a college changed.
✅ Đúng: ...how much a college has changed.

2. Relative Pronouns with Quantifiers (some of which)

Cấu trúc giúp kết nối câu phức, thể hiện khả năng sử dụng ngữ pháp Band 8.0+.

Cụm từ Ý nghĩa
Involve Bao gồm/Liên quan đến
Conversion Sự chuyển đổi mục đích sử dụng
Relocation Sự thay đổi vị trí

3. Vocabulary for Positioning (Spot & Occupy)

Spot

"A cafe could be easily spotted..."

Dùng thay cho "be built" hoặc "be located" để làm bài viết tự nhiên hơn.

Occupy

"The space which used to be occupied by..."

Dùng để miêu tả một phần không gian bị chiếm lĩnh bởi một cơ sở vật chất cụ thể.

Sơ đồ so sánh sự thay đổi kích thước (Reduction)

graph LR A[Original Size] -- "reduced by two times" --> B[50% Size] A -- "halved" --> B A -- "reduction in size" --> B style B fill:#fecaca,stroke:#ef4444
Pro Tip: Khi miêu tả sự giảm kích thước, thay vì chỉ dùng "become smaller", hãy dùng "be reduced by [số] times" hoặc "be halved" để ghi điểm từ vựng Academic.

Exercise 2: Sentence Transformation

Câu 1: Viết lại câu dùng cấu trúc "How much"

The diagrams illustrate the changes a student accommodation has gone through from 2010 to the present.

The diagrams illustrate how much a student accommodation has changed from 2010 to the present.

Câu 2: Dùng "some of which" và từ đồng nghĩa cho "transformation"

...the building has witnessed several changes, and some modifications involve the transformation of some features.

...the building has witnessed several changes, some of which involve the conversion and relocation of some features.
