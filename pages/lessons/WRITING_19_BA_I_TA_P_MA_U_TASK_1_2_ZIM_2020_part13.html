TASK 2

Dạng bài: Cause and Effect (Family & Children)

Đề bài:

In some countries around the world men and women are having children late in life. What are the reasons for this development? What are the effects on society and family life?

graph TD A[Late Parenthood] --> B[Reasons] A --> C[Effects] B --> B1[Career Success & Priorities] B --> B2[Cognitive decline awareness] C --> C1[Society: Aging Population] C --> C2[Family: Dysfunctional risk]

Academic Vocabulary

  • 1
    Cognitive abilities: Khả năng nhận thức
  • 2
    Aging process: Quá trình lão hóa
  • 3
    Postgraduate courses: Khóa học sau đại học
  • 4
    Produce offspring: Sinh con cái

Pro Tip by Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư

Thay vì dùng từ "children" lặp lại, hãy sử dụng "offspring" hoặc "younger generation". Để mô tả tác động tiêu cực, cụm "detrimental effects" hoặc "undesirable consequences" sẽ giúp bạn ghi điểm cao ở tiêu chí Lexical Resource.

Model Answer (Band 8.0+)

Adults nowadays tend to delay the process of starting a family until later in life. This can be attributed to their desire to pursue further education and career development, and I believe this trend has detrimental effects on both society and family life.

To many people, further education and career success are bigger priorities... They try to study as long as they can by enrolling in postgraduate study courses(3)... many young people decide to work around the clock to climb their career ladder. Therefore, they may be too busy to be pregnant, not to mention(4) having to take care of their child after birth.

However, such late parenthood may bring about many undesirable consequences... From a social aspect, a country whose residents delay producing offspring(5) is likely to face an aging population(6). This can be followed by a shortage in the workforce(7)... As for families, childless couples do not have as great motivation to stay married... reared in a dysfunctional family(9) may prevent parents from getting divorced, but without one, it is easier for them to leave.

Export Rice Prices (Thailand vs Vietnam)

Year Thailand (High) Vietnam (Lower)
2012 $500m (Jan) → $550m (Dec) $400m (Stable)
2013-14 Decline to $400m Gradual decline to $325m

Bài giảng của Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư

"Đối với Line Graph có 2 đối tượng, hãy luôn so sánh vị trí của chúng. Thái Lan luôn giữ ngôi vương về giá so với Việt Nam trong cả giai đoạn."

Key Trends:
  • • Overall: Thai rice > Vietnam rice.
  • • Pattern: Significant fluctuation.
  • • Thailand end: $450m (2015).
  • • Vietnam end: $325m (2015).
