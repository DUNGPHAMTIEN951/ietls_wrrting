1. Bài mẫu Task 2: Job Stability vs. Job Hopping

Working at one place can offer employees a more stable income and career prospects. These rewards and policies are usually applied to full-time employees or those who have worked for over a year. Applicants frequently job-hopping stand a lower chance of getting accepted and are more likely to face unemployment in the future.

PRO TIP by Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư: Sử dụng cụm từ "Stand a lower chance of" thay cho "Can't" để tăng tính khách quan (Academic Hedging) trong bài viết Task 2.

On the other hand, changing jobs can help people, especially the young, develop a broader knowledge base. Job hoppers can achieve career advancement without spending years for promotions, but they are required to adapt more quickly and showcase their ability.

Academic Vocabulary List

  • Self-enhancement: Phát triển bản thân
  • Job-hopping: Nhảy việc
  • Stereotypical: Mang tính khuôn mẫu
  • Achieve career advancement: Thăng tiến
  • Showcase ability: Thể hiện năng lực
  • Build a skilled workforce: Xây dựng lực lượng lao động

2. Task 1: Coffee Prices in Australia (2010-2014)

Phân tích bài mẫu (Band 7.0+)

graph TD A[Sydney 2010: $2.8] -->|Up 13.2%| B[Sydney 2014] C[Melbourne] -->|Up 20.8%| D[Most notable rise] E[Brisbane] -->|Constant| F[No price change]

Language of Change

Để miêu tả dữ liệu trong bảng, Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư khuyến khích học viên sử dụng đa dạng các động từ và tính từ chỉ xu hướng.

  • - Remarkable rise: Tăng đáng kể
  • - Most notable change: Thay đổi đáng chú ý nhất
  • - Boosted its price: Tăng giá
"Overall, the price of a cup of coffee in Melbourne experienced the most remarkable rise over the course of time... Brisbane sold 2.25 dollars and was the only city with no price rise."

3. Discussion Essay: Leadership Skills

Quan điểm 1: Lãnh đạo là bẩm sinh

Many people claim that leaders are born with a charismatic personality. These distinctive personal traits enable them to inspire others through their innate charisma or powerful presence.

Teacher's Note: Khi thảo luận về tính bẩm sinh, hãy dùng các tính từ như genetic, innate, inherited.

Quan điểm 2: Lãnh đạo có thể học được

Skills such as communication or problem-solving are learnable and achievable. Through proper training, individuals can reap significant rewards and develop their leadership capabilities.

Practice Time!

Điền từ thích hợp vào chỗ trống để hoàn thiện câu văn Band 8.0+

"Leadership is not always ________; many skills can be ________ through rigorous practice."

Đáp án: innate / acquired

"Frequent ________ can lead to an unstable career path and the risk of facing ________."

Đáp án: job-hopping / unemployment
