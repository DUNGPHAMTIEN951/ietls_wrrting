01

Sự phát triển của Công cụ Đồ đá

Đề bài Phân tích

"The diagram below shows the development of cutting tools in the Stone Age."

Đối tượng: Công cụ cắt (Tool A & B)
Thời gian: 1.4 triệu năm & 0.8 triệu năm trước
Tiêu chí so sánh: Kích thước, độ sắc, bề mặt, tính năng

Teacher's Note: Từ vựng Band 8+

  • Rudimental: Thô sơ, sơ khai.
  • Counterpart: Đối trọng/vật tương ứng (dùng thay cho 'tool').
  • Rugged: Gồ ghề (thay cho 'rough').
  • Piercing: Đâm xuyên.
  • Well-defined: Rõ nét, sắc sảo.
Sample Report (Band 8.0+) by Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư

The picture shows how cutting tools in the Stone Age had developed, with tool A dating 1.4 million years ago, and tool B 0.8 million years ago.

Overall, tool A was much more rudimental, with its edges being less defined, and therefore less sharp, and tool B’s surface was less rugged and it is larger and longer than its earlier counterpart. Both improvements allowed humans to hold it more easily and enhanced its cutting functionality.

Tool A was about 9 cm long, which was approximately 2 cm shorter than tool B. The front and back views of both tools reveal that the latter’s surface was much smoother, and its pointy shape resembled a waterdrop, making it easier to hold at the bigger end and more efficient in piercing food.

The side view clearly indicates that the thickness of both tools was the same. However, the well-defined line running from the top to bottom of the picture means tool B had become much sharper than Tool A.

Chi tiết 2

Phần trên: Thân bóng (envelope), gores, van xả

Sample Report (Band 8.0+) by Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư

The diagram illustrates the working of a hot air balloon.

Overall, it is clear that the envelope is the largest part of the total area. In addition, the diagram indicates that propane is the fuel that is used to produce the hot air.

The Working Principle:

At the base of the hot air balloon, a propane cylinder is located in the basket. The propane then mixes with air in the burner, producing a flame to heat the air. Next, the hot air rises inside the envelope, causing the balloon to rise from the ground, because the hot air is lighter than the cool air in the surrounding atmosphere. Ropes are shown securing the basket section to the skirt of the envelope.

In addition to the skirt at the base of the envelope, gores and panels also form part of the envelope section. A parachute valve at the top of the envelope is a self-sealing flap. This allows the pilot of the balloon to control the rate at which the hot air can escape.

Pro Tip: Cấu trúc câu chỉ Nguyên nhân - Kết quả

Trong dạng Process, việc sử dụng các mệnh đề chỉ kết quả giúp tăng điểm Cohesion:

"...causing the balloon to rise from the ground, because the hot air is lighter..."

Sử dụng phân từ hiện tại (V-ing) sau dấu phẩy là cách viết cực kỳ "đắt" cho Band 8+.

Thực hành & Kiểm tra

Điền từ vựng chuyên sâu đã học vào đoạn văn bên dưới:

The Upper part of an air balloon resembles (giống) an inverted (đảo ngược) onion. Users can control the rate at which hot air escapes (thoát ra) via the parachute valve.

▶ Xem đáp án & Phân tích chi tiết (by Phạm Tiến Dũng Gia Sư)
  • 1. Resembles: Phù hợp khi mô tả hình dáng (Appearance) thay cho "is similar to".
  • 2. Inverted: Thuật ngữ kỹ thuật mô tả vật bị đảo ngược (ví dụ: inverted triangle).
  • 3. Escape: Động từ chính xác cho việc khí hoặc chất lỏng thoát ra từ bình chứa.
